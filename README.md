# Easy Removable Mods for the Casio F-91W ⌚

**By [NODE](https://n-o-d-e.net/watch_mods3.html)**  

This project showcases simple, tool-free modifications for the iconic **Casio F-91W** digital watch.  
All designs are open-source and easy to print or adapt for your own use.

---

## 🧩 Overview

The goal of these mods is to enhance and customize your F-91W without requiring any tools or technical knowledge.  
Both of the featured designs — **Screen Covers** and a **Micro SD Add-On** — can be easily attached or removed, giving you a modular, customizable watch experience.

---

## 🪞 Screen Covers

Changing the face of the F-91W usually requires removing the acrylic front, which risks scratches or damage.  
These **clip-on screen covers** solve that problem.

### ✨ Features
- Snap-on design using the F-91’s natural grooves  
- Instantly change the face without opening the watch  
- Works with both standard and inverted LCDs  
- Allows color and design customization  

### 🎨 Customization Options
You can use various **transparent colored sheets (≤ 0.3 mm thick)** to change the LCD tint or face design:
- Photography/videography color correction gels  
- Model-making transparent plastics  
- DIY window tints or filters  

> ⚠️ Light color tints maintain LCD readability; darker colors may reduce visibility.

### 🖨️ Printing Tips
- Optimized for **0.08 mm layer height**  
- Works well with textured PEI print beds  
- Can be sanded and painted for smooth finishes  
- Suitable for **UV printing** custom face art  

### 🔒 Protection
When the cover is not in use, a **clip-on protector** prevents the legs from breaking during storage.

---

## 💾 Micro SD Add-On

A redesigned **Micro SD Card module** that magnetically attaches to the back of the F-91W — no screws or seal breaking required.

### ⚙️ Features
- Magnetic attachment to the backplate bulge  
- Easy to remove and re-attach  
- Maintains water resistance  
- Securely holds a Micro SD card for files, backups, or projects  

### 🧲 Magnet Specs
- Designed for **4 × 8 × 1 mm** magnets  
- Can be adapted for other 1 mm thick magnets  

### 💧 Water Resistance
Since the watch remains sealed and SD cards are waterproof, your watch retains full water resistance even with the add-on attached.
